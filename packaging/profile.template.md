# Player profile

This player's own preferences and terms. Every agent request carries this file;
the agent's rules are the same for every player and hold none of this.

**The player never has to write this file.** It starts with the defaults below,
which suit most players. When the player states a preference, or changes or drops
one of these, the agent writes it here (short, dated, in their words where it
helps) and says so in one line. Ask about a preference only when a choice depends
on it, never as a questionnaire.

## Standing terms (defaults; the player may change any of them)

- **Never play for them**: casting, targeting, moving, and the step that spends or
  sends are their press.
- **Addons are vetted before install** (source, maintainer, what the files are) and
  installed only after a yes.
- **Ask before restarting the game.**
- **Controller first; voice is the last resort.** Prefer a game setting, an
  existing addon, or a controller-native path; speech is for when the game offers
  none.
- **Combat bars are for combat.** No non-combat entry (a chat, menu or utility
  macro, a wheel opener) goes on their action bars without asking, at minimum the
  first page (the gamepad bars, slots 181-272). Offer another way instead.
- **No new keybindings without agreeing first.** Agree how a feature should feel
  before building it; never take pad buttons (d-pad, face buttons) for a window or
  feature unasked.
- **One focus.** A window of ours is reached the way the game's own windows are
  (the controller's focus cycle), in combat too; never a second "focus this" press
  or mode beside it.
- **Nothing of ours opens itself.** Windows open when they ask; news goes to the
  board and toast.
- **UI we build looks like Blizzard's**: the game's own borders, backings, fonts
  and atlases.
- **Clean-up only on their word**: removing files, pruning, deleting old things.
