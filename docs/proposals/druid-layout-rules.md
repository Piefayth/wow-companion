# Pietra's layout rules (the player's words, gathered 2026-11-09; correct me here)

Every layout is derived from these and checked against them before it is sent.

1. Every castable spell has a home (bar, wheel or macro), except ones the player
   drops: Attack, Cultivation.
2. No duplicates: a spell lives in exactly one place.
3. Forms come from the native L1+L2 wheel; no form-shift buttons.
4. Help/harm macros to save space. Caster, no target: the friendly spell on self.
   In a form, no target: the form ability.
5. Pair by role across forms: a caster attack shares a button with feral attacks;
   never a cat or bear attack on the same button as a caster heal.
6. Button value: L2/R2 face buttons first (pressable while moving), then the
   L2/R2 d-pads, then L2+R2 for situational and long-cooldown things (crowd
   control, Rebirth). Naked d-pad: below the modifier bars.
7. Heals where they were: L2 triangle, circle, cross.
8. Crowd control is situational: L2+R2.
9. Wheels: Buffs (Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Teleport: Moonglade); Items (only
   usable bag and quest items). Low priority.
10. War Stomp on R2 triangle, usable in every form (to try).
11. Stance page on for R2 (GamepadStanceBarOverride 3): plain spells per form page.
12. Plain spells wherever possible; macros only where nothing else works (wheels).

## Tried and rejected (the final layout must avoid every one)

- Form-shift buttons (GoCat, GoBear): the native L1+L2 wheel already does it.
- "Help/harm" heals that only chose friend or self, with no enemy branch.
- The same heal on two bars (L2 heals plus R2 help/harm heals; a second Regrowth).
- Consumables and racials on the naked d-pad; later, wheels on the naked d-pad.
- Heals on a d-pad: the left thumb is moving.
- Buffs folded into attack macros (Thorns, Mark of the Wild on a friend branch),
  and the Buffs wheel stripped of Thorns as a result.
- Crowd control moved off L2+R2 onto main buttons.
- An Items wheel that listed things you cannot use.
- Cat and bear attacks on the same button as caster heals, so caster attacks
  look absent in caster form.
- Attack, Cultivation, Teleport: Moonglade on bar slots.
- Macro icons showing "?".
- A layout that left a spell out (Feral Charge (Cat), the first time).
- Macros where a plain spell would do: once help/harm went, the native stance
  page already swaps R2 per form, so every HH_ macro was clutter with a name label.
